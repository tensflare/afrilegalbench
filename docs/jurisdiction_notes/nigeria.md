# Nigeria — Jurisdiction Notes

## Legal System

Nigeria operates a pluralistic legal system:
- **English common law** — received via colonial legacy, governs most commercial and civil matters
- **Customary law** — recognized where not repugnant to natural justice, equity, and good conscience
- **Sharia law** — applies in 12 northern states for Muslim personal status matters

## Court hierarchy

1. Supreme Court (final appellate)
2. Court of Appeal
3. Federal High Court / State High Courts
4. Customary Court of Appeal / Sharia Court of Appeal
5. Magistrates' Courts / Customary Courts / Sharia Courts

## Languages

English is the official language of court proceedings. Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo, and 400+ other languages spoken.
