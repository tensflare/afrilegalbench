# South Africa — Jurisdiction Notes

## Legal System

South Africa has a hybrid legal system:
- **Roman-Dutch law** — common law foundation
- **English common law** — influence on procedure and commercial law
- **Customary law** — recognized under the Constitution

## Court hierarchy

1. Constitutional Court (highest for constitutional matters)
2. Supreme Court of Appeal
3. High Courts (divisions per province)
4. Magistrates' Courts
5. Traditional courts / Small Claims Courts

## Languages

11 official languages. English is primary for court records but proceedings may be in any official language.

## Data sources

- SAFLII (saflii.org) — excellent free access, largest collection of African case law online
- Government statutes (gov.za) — public domain

## Relevant statutes

- Constitution of the Republic of South Africa, 1996
- Interpretation Act 33 of 1957
- Companies Act 71 of 2008
- National Credit Act 34 of 2005
- Labour Relations Act 66 of 1995
- Recognition of Customary Marriages Act 120 of 1998
