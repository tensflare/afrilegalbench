# Kenya — Jurisdiction Notes

## Legal System

Kenya operates a pluralistic legal system:
- **English common law** — received via colonial legacy
- **Customary law** — recognized under the Constitution for personal status matters
- **Islamic law** — applies to Muslim personal status matters under the Kadhis' Courts

## Court hierarchy

1. Supreme Court
2. Court of Appeal
3. High Court (including specialized divisions: Constitutional, Commercial, Family, etc.)
4. Magistrates' Courts
5. Kadhis' Courts (Islamic law)
6. Courts Martial

## Languages

English is the official language of court proceedings. Swahili is the national language.

## Data sources

- KenyaLII (kenyalaw.org) — free case law and legislation
- SAFLII (saflii.org) — also hosts Kenyan content

## Relevant statutes

- Constitution of Kenya 2010
- Evidence Act (Cap 80)
- Companies Act 2015
- Marriage Act 2014
- Land Registration Act 2012
- Children Act 2022
